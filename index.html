Eastern Christian Tradition

The Jesus Prayer

Central to Orthodox and Eastern Catholic hesychast spirituality, traditionally repeated many times on a knotted prayer rope — a komboskini or chotki, usually 33, 50, 100, or 300 knots, not a rosary — and traditionally received from a spiritual father rather than taken up alone.
